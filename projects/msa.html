Mohawk Students’ Association

Marketing Research & Strategy

Market ResearchFocus GroupSurveyConsumer InsightsStrategy

The challenge

Explore how Mohawk students view and engage with the MSA, identify barriers to participation, and find ways to increase awareness and involvement.

Research approach

The team used a student focus group and survey research to examine awareness of MSA services, perceived value, engagement barriers, communication habits and preferred social platforms.

Key findings

Schedule conflicts and lack of interest were major barriers. Students also preferred practical, relevant communication through channels such as email, social media and word-of-mouth.

Strategic direction

Recommendations focused on more personal outreach, stronger short-form content, clearer promotion of services and events, and more relevant opportunities for student connection and career growth.

Academic team project completed at Mohawk College. This page highlights the project scope, research and strategic direction.
