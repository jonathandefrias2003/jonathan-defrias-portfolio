Project scope

Built a strategic marketing plan for the Dyson 360 Heurist robotic vacuum.

Target market

The plan focused on tech-oriented consumers and positioned AI household mapping as a key product benefit.

Marketing tactics

Recommendations included influencer marketing, retail demonstrations, out-of-home advertising, event activation and a recycling incentive.

Measurement

The plan considered metrics such as sales revenue, customer acquisition cost and return on investment.
