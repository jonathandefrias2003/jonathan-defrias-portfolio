Dyson 360 Heurist

A comprehensive marketing plan connecting a technology product to its audience, channels and retail moments.

Project scope

Built a strategic marketing plan for the Dyson 360 Heurist robotic vacuum.

DisciplineComprehensive Marketing Plan
AudienceTech-oriented consumers
ChannelsInfluencer, retail, OOH and events
01 / Target market

Position the intelligence, not just the machine.

The plan focused on tech-oriented consumers and positioned AI household mapping as a key product benefit.

02 / Marketing tactics

Connect discovery to demonstration.

Recommendations included influencer marketing, retail demonstrations, out-of-home advertising, event activation and a recycling incentive.

03 / Measurement

Give the plan a practical scorecard.

The plan considered metrics such as sales revenue, customer acquisition cost and return on investment.

This case study highlights an academic strategic plan and its proposed measurement approach.
